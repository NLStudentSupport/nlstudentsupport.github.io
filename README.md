# NL Student Support

A free resource to help students in the Netherlands find mental health and support services at their universities.

**Website:** https://nlstudentsupport.github.io

## Features

- Directory of 60+ Dutch higher education institutions
- Search and filter by institution type
- Find support services by category:
  - Mental Health / Psychologist
  - Student Counsellor
  - Study Adviser
  - Disability Support
  - Financial Support
  - General Wellbeing
  - Crisis Support
- Direct links to official university support pages
- National support resources including crisis lines
- Dedicated support page for Iranian students

## Usage

Visit the website, select your university from the directory, and choose the type of support you need. You'll be provided with direct links to official university resources.
